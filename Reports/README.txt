========================================================================
OptiAssist - Maintenance Reports Folder (Reports)
========================================================================

This folder is used to store copies of service orders and
maintenance records performed on client computers.

Each generated report contains:
- Service Provider and Contact Info
- Client Name and Order Number
- Execution Date and Timestamp
- Complete Device Specifications (OS, CPU, RAM, GPU, Disks, TPM, Secure Boot)
- Performed Procedures and Services Checklist
- Technical Assessment and Observations
========================================================================
